❯ what does HDA mean

⏺ HDA = Highway Driving Assist — Hyundai/Kia's ADAS system
  that combines adaptive cruise control (ACC) + lane
  centering into one integrated system.

  - HDA 1 — ACC + lane centering on divided highways
  - HDA 2 — adds lane change assist (tap turn signal → car
  changes lanes automatically)

  In the research, HDA (any version) is what satisfies the
  "ACC + auto lane centering" requirement for this car
  search.
